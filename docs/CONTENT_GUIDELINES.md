# Content Guidelines

IdeaByHuman is a curated showcase of projects built with AI. We celebrate creative collaboration between human vision and artificial intelligence.

## What Qualifies

### Core Criteria
- **Significantly AI-built**: The project must demonstrate substantial use of AI in development (not just AI-assisted tools)
- **Functional & Complete**: The project should be live and usable, not a concept or demo
- **Novel Application**: Shows creativity in how AI was used, not just applying existing solutions
- **Human Vision**: Started with human creativity, problem-solving, or vision
- **Genuine Value**: Solves a real problem or creates something meaningful

### Project Types
We feature projects across these categories:

1. **Web Applications** - Full-stack apps, SaaS products, web tools
2. **Mobile Apps** - iOS, Android, cross-platform applications
3. **Content Tools** - Writing, editing, generation, and content creation
4. **Automation** - Workflow automation, productivity tools, scrapers
5. **Creative Projects** - Art, design, music, creative tools
6. **Data & Analytics** - Visualization, dashboards, analytics platforms
7. **E-commerce** - Stores, marketplaces, shopping platforms

## Required Elements

Every submitted project must include:

### Essential Information
- **Title**: Clear, compelling project name
- **Short Description**: 1-2 sentences explaining what it does (max 150 chars)
- **Live URL**: Where people can access the project
- **Category**: Primary category (from list above)
- **Hero Image**: Eye-catching cover image (1200x630px recommended)

### The Story (why reviewers care)
Your project's story has four parts:

1. **The Problem**: What problem did you identify? What gap in the market or gap in your own workflow did this fill? (200-300 words)

2. **Your Idea**: What was your creative vision? How did you approach solving this? What makes your approach unique? (200-300 words)

3. **AI's Role**: How did you use AI to build this? Which AI tools and models? What did you prompt? What surprised you? How did you iterate? (300-400 words)

4. **Surprises & Learnings**: What went differently than expected? What did you learn about AI or building products? Honest reflections are valuable. (200-300 words)

### Technical Details
- **Live Link**: Fully functional, publicly accessible URL
- **GitHub URL** (optional): Link to code repository
- **Demo Video** (optional): YouTube or Vimeo link showing project in action
- **Tech Stack**: 5-10 key technologies used (Next.js, Claude API, Supabase, etc.)
- **AI Tools Used**: Specific models and services (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, ChatGPT-4, Midjourney, etc.)
- **Build Time**: Approximate hours spent building

### Media Assets
- **Hero Image**: Main showcase image (required)
- **Screenshots**: 2-5 additional images showing the project (recommended)
- **Demo Video**: Short walkthrough or highlight reel (optional but encouraged)

## Story Structure Best Practices

### Problem Section
- Start with a specific, relatable scenario
- Avoid generic problems ("make things easier")
- Show genuine user research or personal validation
- Example: "Calendar apps force me to re-enter events in three places..."

### Idea Section
- Explain your unique angle
- What existing solutions did you consider?
- Why did you decide to build it?
- Example: "Instead of yet another calendar, I wanted to build something that learns your schedule patterns..."

### AI's Role Section
- Be specific about which models and APIs you used
- Share actual prompts or techniques (if not proprietary)
- Explain iteration & refinement
- Honest about limitations: "Claude excelled at X, but struggled with Y..."
- Example: "I used Claude API to generate schedule suggestions, testing 20+ prompt variations..."

### Surprises Section
- Share unexpected discoveries
- Real challenges you faced
- How you overcame them
- Honest reflection on AI capabilities/limitations
- Example: "I expected AI to handle context-switching perfectly, but learned it needed explicit reset prompts..."

## What Doesn't Qualify

### ❌ Will Be Rejected
- **No AI involvement**: Projects built without meaningful AI use (regular web apps, etc.)
- **Proof-of-concept only**: Early-stage, non-functional projects
- **Incomplete**: Missing hero image, story, or broken live links
- **Copied/Cloned**: Projects that are direct copies of existing solutions without significant modification
- **Duplicate submission**: Resubmitting a previously rejected project without substantial changes
- **Commercial spam**: Products with sole purpose of selling or marketing
- **Explicit/adult content**: NSFW, adult entertainment, or harmful content
- **Illegal activities**: Tools designed to facilitate illegal activities

### ⚠️ Red Flags (May Require Clarification)
- Very recent launches (within 24 hours) - reviewers want to see stability
- No actual AI involvement visible - needs clear explanation of AI's role
- Stories that are vague about AI usage
- Poor image quality or broken demo links
- Minimal effort in writing the story sections

## Story Tone & Voice

Write your story as if explaining to a fellow builder:

- **Honest**: Include challenges and learnings, not just successes
- **Specific**: Concrete examples, not generic statements
- **Reflective**: Why did you make certain choices?
- **Accessible**: Avoid heavy jargon; explain AI concepts clearly
- **Conversational**: Like you're chatting with a friend over coffee

### Examples of Good Story Excerpts

**Problem** (Specific & Relatable):
> "Every morning I spent 15 minutes transcribing voice notes into my project management system. I'd often forget details, and the context was always lost. I needed something that could understand my voice, extract action items, and integrate with my existing workflows."

**AI's Role** (Specific & Transparent):
> "I used Whisper API for transcription, then piped the text into Claude with a carefully crafted prompt that extracted action items, assigned priorities, and created Slack messages. The key insight was giving Claude clear examples of my preferred output format."

**Surprises** (Genuine & Reflective):
> "I expected the hardest part to be getting the transcription right, but the real challenge was prompt engineering. My first prompts were too generic. By showing Claude 10 examples of transcriptions I'd written manually, the output improved dramatically."

## Submission Checklist

Before hitting submit, verify:

- [ ] Project is live and fully functional
- [ ] Live URL works and is publicly accessible
- [ ] Hero image is high quality (1200x630px or larger)
- [ ] All story sections are completed (Problem, Idea, AI's Role, Surprises)
- [ ] Story sections total 900-1300 words
- [ ] AI tools and models are clearly listed
- [ ] Tech stack includes 5+ technologies
- [ ] No broken links (GitHub, demo video, live URL)
- [ ] Content follows tone guidelines (honest, specific, conversational)
- [ ] Screenshots included (2-5 recommended)

## Review Timeline

- **Submission**: Project enters "pending" status
- **Review Window**: Typically reviewed within 7-10 days
- **Feedback**: You'll be notified of approval, rejection, or request for changes
- **Revisions**: If changes requested, you have 7 days to resubmit
- **Publication**: Approved projects go live in gallery within 24 hours

## Questions?

If you're unsure whether your project qualifies, reach out before submitting. We're happy to discuss your project and provide feedback on the story before formal submission.
