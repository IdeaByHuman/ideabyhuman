# Brand Guidelines

How we talk about and present IdeaByHuman.

## Brand Promise

**Where human vision meets AI capability**

IdeaByHuman celebrates the creative fusion of human imagination and artificial intelligence. We showcase projects that push boundaries, challenge assumptions, and demonstrate what's possible when humans and AI collaborate thoughtfully.

## Core Values

### Authenticity
We value honest storytelling over hype. Real challenges, genuine learnings, and transparent reflections matter more than perfect success stories.

### Creativity
We celebrate novel thinking and creative problem-solving. We're not interested in AI for its own sake—only in AI used creatively to build things that matter.

### Community
We believe in the power of shared learning. Creators lift each other up. We celebrate success together and learn from failures together.

### Quality
We're curated, not crowded. Every project on IdeaByHuman meets real standards. Quality over quantity, always.

## Brand Voice & Tone

### How We Talk

**Authentic** - Honest, not corporate-speak
- ✅ "We struggled to get the AI to understand context, so we tried 20 different prompts"
- ❌ "Our AI leverages advanced NLP paradigms for context optimization"

**Inspiring** - We celebrate human creativity and ambition
- ✅ "What these builders created is genuinely impressive"
- ❌ "Obviously AI is better than humans"

**Accessible** - Clear language for technical and non-technical audiences
- ✅ "Claude helps generate ideas, then humans refine them"
- ❌ "Leveraging transformer-based LLM architectures for ideation"

**Encouraging** - We uplift creators and welcome experimentation
- ✅ "We'd love to see your project, even if it's still early"
- ❌ "Only submit if your project meets these stringent criteria"

**Reflective** - We think deeply about technology and its implications
- ✅ "AI is a tool that amplifies human creativity when used thoughtfully"
- ❌ "AI will solve everything"

### Tone Examples

**For project approvals:**
"Congrats on [Project]! We were impressed by your creative approach to using AI. The fact that you iterated on your prompts shows genuine understanding. Can't wait to see what you build next!"

**For rejection feedback:**
"We loved the core idea, but the story section felt a bit rushed. We'd like to see you expand on how you actually used AI in the build process. Resubmit with more detail, and we'll review again!"

**For community communication:**
"This month we're featuring projects built in a single weekend. Shows what's possible when you move fast and think creatively. Inspired to build something? Submit your project!"

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette (TBD)
Brand colors to be defined during design phase. Considerations:

- **Primary**: Modern, tech-forward, inviting
- **Secondary**: Complements primary, good contrast
- **Accent**: Draws attention to key actions (submit, feature)
- **Neutral**: Clean backgrounds, readable text

Guidelines will be:
- Accessible (WCAG AA standard minimum)
- Work in both light and dark modes
- Professional yet approachable

### Typography

**Headings**: Bold, modern, confident
- Should convey innovation and quality
- Hierarchy clear and easy to scan

**Body Text**: Legible, comfortable to read
- Clear, sans-serif font
- Generous line spacing
- High contrast with backgrounds

**Code/Technical**: Monospace font for clarity
- Used in project tech stacks
- Used in documentation

### Visual Style

**Images**:
- High-quality, real project screenshots
- Candid, not overly polished
- Diverse projects and creators
- Authentic, not stock photos

**Icons**:
- Consistent style across platform
- Help with navigation and categorization
- Accessible with label/title attributes

**Spacing & Layout**:
- Generous whitespace
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Mobile-responsive
- Accessible focus states

## Communication Channels

### Voice by Channel

**Website/Product**:
- Helpful and clear
- Inspiring without being cheesy
- Respectful of user time

**Email**:
- Conversational but professional
- Personal (not automated-sounding)
- Always add value

**Social Media**:
- Personality and humor are OK
- Real engagement, not promotions
- Celebrate creator wins

**Documentation**:
- Clear and comprehensive
- Walkthrough, not just reference
- Use examples liberally

**Customer Support**:
- Friendly and helpful
- Patient with all questions
- Genuine care about solving problems

## Dos & Don'ts

### Do ✅
- Tell honest stories, including failures
- Celebrate diverse projects and creators
- Use clear, accessible language
- Give credit to creators
- Admit when you're learning
- Show personality

### Don't ❌
- Oversell AI capabilities
- Use corporate jargon
- Make it seem easy (building is hard)
- Exclude non-technical people
- Claim AI is better than humans
- Be overly formal or stiff

## Brand Evolution

This guide will evolve as the community grows and we learn what resonates. The core principle remains:

**Authentic storytelling about real people using AI to build remarkable things.**
