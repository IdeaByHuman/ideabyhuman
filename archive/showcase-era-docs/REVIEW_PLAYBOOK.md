# Review Playbook

Guide for human reviewers evaluating project submissions.

## Reviewer Responsibilities

As a reviewer, you're the quality gate for IdeaByHuman. Your role is to:

1. **Validate qualifications** - Ensure projects meet our core criteria
2. **Assess storytelling** - Check that the submission tells a compelling, honest story
3. **Evaluate AI integration** - Confirm substantial & thoughtful AI usage
4. **Provide constructive feedback** - Help creators improve if needed
5. **Maintain curation standards** - Keep the platform high-quality

## Evaluation Criteria

Rate each project on these dimensions:

### 1. Originality & Creativity (1-10)
**What to assess:**
- Does the project show novel thinking or unique approach?
- Is this a copy of existing solutions?
- How creative is the application of AI?
- Does it solve a real problem in a new way?

**Rating Guide:**
- **9-10**: Genuinely novel idea with creative AI usage
- **7-8**: Good execution of a solid concept
- **5-6**: Functional but somewhat derivative
- **3-4**: Limited originality, mostly applies existing patterns
- **1-2**: Appears to be copy/clone of existing solution

### 2. Execution Quality (1-10)
**What to assess:**
- Is the project functional and complete?
- Are there obvious bugs or incomplete features?
- Does the UI/UX show care and polish?
- Does it work as described?

**Rating Guide:**
- **9-10**: Polished, professional-quality execution
- **7-8**: Well-built with minor issues
- **5-6**: Functional but rough around edges
- **3-4**: Several bugs or missing features
- **1-2**: Barely functional or broken

### 3. Story Quality (1-10)
**What to assess:**
- Is the story compelling and well-written?
- Does it clearly explain the problem?
- Is the creator's thinking transparent?
- Are there honest reflections and learnings?
- Is the AI's role clearly explained?

**Rating Guide:**
- **9-10**: Exceptional storytelling, genuine insights, compelling narrative
- **7-8**: Clear story with good detail and honesty
- **5-6**: Adequate story but lacks depth or specificity
- **3-4**: Vague or generic story, unclear AI role
- **1-2**: Minimal effort, barely coherent story

### 4. AI Integration (1-10)
**What to assess:**
- How substantially was AI used in development?
- Does the AI usage feel integral or tacked on?
- Is the AI tool choice appropriate for the problem?
- Did the creator demonstrate understanding of AI limitations?

**Rating Guide:**
- **9-10**: AI is core to the solution; thoughtful integration; clear understanding
- **7-8**: Substantial AI usage; mostly appropriate tool choices
- **5-6**: Meaningful AI involvement but not critical to success
- **3-4**: Minor AI usage; feels supplementary
- **1-2**: Questionable whether AI was meaningfully used

## Approval Checklist

A project should meet these criteria for approval:

### Must-Have Requirements
- [ ] Project is live and functional at provided URL
- [ ] All required fields completed (title, description, story, image)
- [ ] Hero image is clear and high-quality
- [ ] Story sections are present and substantial (300+ words total minimum)
- [ ] AI tools and technologies clearly listed
- [ ] No broken links (demo, GitHub, etc.)
- [ ] No explicit, adult, or harmful content
- [ ] Not a clear duplicate of existing solution

### Quality Thresholds
- [ ] **Originality score**: ≥ 5/10
- [ ] **Execution score**: ≥ 6/10
- [ ] **Story score**: ≥ 6/10
- [ ] **AI Integration score**: ≥ 6/10

### Total Score Calculation
Average of four scores. **Approval threshold: 6.0 or higher**

**Decision Matrix:**
- **8.0-10.0**: Feature-worthy (discuss with team for featured status)
- **6.0-7.9**: Approve and add to gallery
- **5.0-5.9**: Borderline - request changes or reject
- **Below 5.0**: Reject

## Red Flags & Concerns

### Automatic Rejection
Stop reviewing immediately and reject if:
- ❌ Project is non-functional or link is broken
- ❌ Explicit, adult, or harmful content
- ❌ Illegal activity or facilitation of illegal acts
- ❌ Clear copyright infringement or plagiarism
- ❌ Duplicates existing approved submission

### Caution Flags (Require Clarification)
These require explanation before approval:
- ⚠️ Project launched less than 24 hours ago (often signs of incomplete work)
- ⚠️ AI involvement is unclear or vague in the story
- ⚠️ Story sections are significantly under-written
- ⚠️ Creator claims extensive AI usage but live product shows none
- ⚠️ No evidence of actual AI integration (just AI-assisted code, not AI-core)
- ⚠️ Project appears to be a course/template project, not original creation

## Featured Project Selection

Projects scoring 8.0+ are candidates for featuring on the homepage. Consider:

- **Exceptional storytelling** - Does the story inspire and educate?
- **Technical achievement** - Is this technically impressive?
- **Accessibility** - Can general audiences understand and use it?
- **Visual appeal** - Are the images compelling?
- **Trend alignment** - Does it showcase timely AI applications?

Featured projects rotate monthly. Team selects 3-5 projects per month.

## Review Process

1. **Submission arrives** → Auto-email to all reviewers
2. **Reviewer evaluates** → Complete scoring & notes within 48 hours (target)
3. **Score threshold met?**
   - **Yes (6.0+)**: Approve
   - **No (5.0-5.9)**: Team discussion - request changes or reject
   - **Below 5.0**: Reject with feedback
4. **Creator notified** → Email with decision & feedback
5. **If resubmission**: Re-review within 48 hours
6. **Publication**: Live in gallery within 24 hours of approval

## Review Tips

### Best Practices
- **Test thoroughly**: Click all links, try the live product, watch videos
- **Read the entire story**: Understand the creator's journey
- **Consider context**: A solo builder has different constraints than a team
- **Be constructive**: Feedback should help them improve, not just criticize
- **Take notes**: Document your scores and reasoning
- **Discuss edge cases**: Use Slack/meetings for borderline projects

### What Makes Good Feedback
- Specific: Point to exact issues, not vague critiques
- Actionable: Explain what they need to change, not just what's wrong
- Respectful: Remember a human created this
- Balanced: Acknowledge strengths alongside areas for improvement

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Rejecting based on personal taste rather than criteria
- Being overly harsh or discouraging
- Approving poor-quality submissions to be nice
- Not testing the project thoroughly
- Allowing scoring bias (first impression, author reputation, etc.)

## FAQs for Reviewers

**Q: What if the creator used AI to build the entire project?**
A: That's perfectly fine! The requirement is meaningful AI usage, not a minimum threshold of human-written code.

**Q: What if I can't access the live project (region block, etc.)?**
A: Request access from the creator, or note the issue and provide feedback on the other aspects. Don't penalize them for inaccessibility you can't control.

**Q: What if the project is using proprietary/paid AI tools I don't have access to?**
A: Evaluate based on the story, documentation, and any visible results. The creator's explanation of their process is sufficient.

**Q: Can I suggest the creator use different AI tools?**
A: Yes, if it's relevant feedback. For example, "Have you considered using Claude for this task? It might improve..." But don't require specific tools.

**Q: What if I disagree with another reviewer?**
A: Discuss in Slack/meetings. If you have significantly different scores (>2 points), have a conversation before finalizing the decision.

**Q: How do I handle a resubmission?**
A: Re-review the same criteria, but give weight to whether they addressed your feedback. If they fixed the issues, approve it.

## Consistency & Standards

We maintain a shared document tracking approvals/rejections to ensure consistency. Review it monthly and discuss:
- Any projects we approved that we'd reject now (standards drift)
- Any trends in rejections (are we too strict? Too lenient?)
- Featured selections and reasoning
- New guidelines or updates needed

Quality and consistency matter more than speed. When in doubt, discuss with the team before deciding.
